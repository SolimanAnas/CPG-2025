    How this simulation builds it

    Study aid only. Waveforms are synthesised from a teaching model of the cardiac dipole; they are calibrated to standard ECG paper (25 mm/s, 10 mm/mV) but are not recordings, not a diagnostic device and not a substitute for clinical assessment. Treat the patient, not the monitor.

    Management content follows the AHA Guidelines for CPR and Emergency Cardiovascular Care (2020) and the 2025 update, and the Fourth Universal Definition of MI for ST criteria. Local protocols and medical direction take precedence. Disclaimer
