M36                  Salbutamol

 Scope of Practice       EMT

 Classification          • Bronchodilator (Beta-2 selective adrenergic agonist)
 Usual
                         • 2.5mg/2.5ml nebule
 Presentation
 Mechanism of
                         • Binds with B-2 adrenergic receptors
 Action
                         • Bronchospasm associated with:
                               • Asthma
                               • COPD
 Indications
                               • Anaphylaxis
                               • Post Drowning/water inhalation
                         • Tocolysis associated with prolapsed umbilical cord


 Contraindications       • Known allergy to salbutamol


                         • Hypertension
 Precautions             • Tachyarrhythmias
                         • Last trimester pregnancy
                         • Angina
 Side Effects            • Tachycardia
                         • Tachyarrhythmias


 Adult Dose
                         • 5mg Nebulized, repeat as required.
 & Administration


                         • <5 years: 2.5mg nebulized, repeat as required.
 Pediatric Dose
                         • >5 years: 5mg nebulized, repeat as required.
 & Administration
                         • Dilute dose with normal saline or sterile water to a maximum of 5ml.
                         • Pregnancy: Safety has not been established. However, salbutamol should be
 Use in Pregnancy          administered if clinically indicated.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Safety has not been established. However, salbutamol should be
                           administered if clinically indicated.

 Additional
                         • Nil.
