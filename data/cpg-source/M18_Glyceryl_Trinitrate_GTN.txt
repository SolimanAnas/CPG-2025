M18                  Glyceryl Trinitrate (GTN)

 Scope of Practice         EMT

 Classification           • Organic Nitrate / Vasodilator
 Usual
                          • 0.4mg (per spray) spray bottle
 Presentation
                          • GTN acts upon vascular smooth muscle to cause venous dilation (and arterial
                            dilation to a lesser extent).
                          • This results in:
                                  • Reduced venous return (preload) this reduces ventricular filling and cardiac
 Mechanism of
                                    output which reduces myocardial oxygen demand
 Action
                                  • Reduced peripheral resistance (afterload) through arterial dilation. This
                                    reduces myocardial oxygen demand through reducing the force the
                                    ventricles must overcome to eject blood.
                                  • Dilation to coronary arteries which may increase myocardial blood flow.
                          • Cardiac Chest Pain
 Indications
                          • Pulmonary edema
                          • Known allergy to corn or corn products
                          • SBP <90mmHg
 Contraindications        • Use of Phosphodiesterase inhibitors within the last 24 hours (e.g. Viagra)
                          • Right ventricular infarct
                          • Heart rate <50 or >150
 Precautions              • Physiologically unstable patients / patients with signs of poor perfusion.
                          • Hypotension
                          • Headache
 Side Effects
                          • Tachycardia
                          • Lightheaded feeling


 Adult Dose               Chest Pain & Pulmonary Edema
 & Administration         • SL: 0.4mg(1x spray) every 3-5 minutes (max 1.2mg/ 3x sprays)


 Pediatric Dose
                          • Not for pediatric administration
 & Administration

 Use in Pregnancy         • Safety has not been established. The likelihood of myocardial infarction or
 and Lactation              ischemia in pregnancy or lactation is very low, GTN should generally be withheld

 Additional                 sub-therapeutic dosing.
 Information              • Consider performing a Right Sided 12-Lead ECG to rule out right ventricular
