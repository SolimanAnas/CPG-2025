M5                   Atropine

 Scope of Practice        EMT-A

 Classification          • Anticholinergic
 Usual
                         • 0.5mg/5ml (prefilled syringe)
 Presentation
                         • Atropine blocks muscarinic acetylcholine receptors reducing vagal tone.
                         • This results in:
 Mechanism of
                                • Increased heartrate
 Action
                                • Drying of salivary and bronchial secretions
                                • Bronchodilation
                         • Compromised Bradycardia.
 Indications
                         • Organophosphate poisoning.



 Contraindications       • Known allergy


                         • Myocardial Ischemia – Atropine will increase myocardial oxygen demand.
 Precautions
                         • Glaucoma – Atropine will increase intraocular pressure.
                         • Tachycardia and Palpitations
                         • Confusion
 Side Effects
                         • Dry mouth
                         • Blurred vision
                         Bradycardia
                         • 1mg IV every 3-5 minutes.
 Adult Dose
                         Organophosphate Poisoning
 & Administration
                         • 2mg IV Initial Dose
                         • Double each subsequent dose (e.g. 4mg/6mg/8mg) until resolution of symptoms.
                         Bradycardia
 Pediatric Dose          • 0.02mg/kg IV/IO every 3-5 minutes.
 & Administration        Organophosphate Poisoning
                         • 0.02-0.05mg/kg IV/IO every 3-5 minutes.
                         • Pregnancy: Fetal harm has been demonstrated. However, atropine should not be
                           withheld if the patient is significantly compromised. Transcutaneous pacing is
 Use in Pregnancy
                           preferred if available.
 and Lactation
                         • Lactation: Infant harm cannot be ruled out. Atropine may be administered, the
                           mother should be advised to stop breast feeding until advised by their physician.

 Additional              • Administer as a fast IV push.
 Information             • Slow administration may result in further (transient) bradycardia.
