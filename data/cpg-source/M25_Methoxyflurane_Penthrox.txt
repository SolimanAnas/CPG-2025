M25                  Methoxyflurane (Penthrox)

 Scope of Practice        EMT

 Classification          • Inhaled Anesthetic
 Usual
                         • 3ml vial
 Presentation
                         • Depresses the central nervous system primarily through enhancing GABA-A
 Mechanism of
                           receptor activity to increase inhibitory neurotransmission.
 Action
                         • Inhibits NMDA receptors to reduce excitatory pain signals.
                         • Moderate to Severe Pain
 Indications
                         • Moderate Pain with occasional Severe Pain associated with procedures
                         • Patients unable to obey commands, or are unable to self-administer
                         • History of malignant hyperthermia (rare)
 Contraindications       • Known renal impairment
                         • Severe liver disease
                         • Hypersensivity to anesthetics
                         • Age >75years – elderly patients are more likely to have renal impairment.
 Precautions
                         • Pre-eclampsia – renal impairment is likely in pre-eclampsia.
                         • Dizziness
 Side Effects            • Headache
                         • Mild and temporary sedation


 Adult Dose              • 3mls Methoxyflurane self administered (repeat dose once if required)
 & Administration        • Adult maximum dose in 24hours is 6ml (2 doses)


                         • Pediatric Maximum dose 3ml (single dose)
                         • Generally, pediatrics should be greater than 5 years old to understand how to self-
                           administer methoxyflurane.
 Pediatric Dose          • Administration in pediatrics has not been widely studied. However, methoxyflurane
 & Administration          is widely administered in the pre-hospital setting with minimal reported adverse
                           effects.
                         • When administered to pediatrics, methoxyflurane should be administered
                           sparingly and more appropriate analgesics should be used once they are available.
                         • Pregnancy: Safety has not been established. Administration during labor is not
 Use in Pregnancy          recommended.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Safety has not been established. If essential to be administered, the
                           mother should be advised to stop breast feeding until advised by their physician.
                         • Methoxyflurane is an effective analgesic provided adequate coaching is provided.
 Additional              • The analgesic effect of methoxyflurane will wear off approximately 30 seconds
 Information               after inhalation has stopped. Constant inhalation is required to provide on-going
                           analgesia.
