M30                  Nefopam

 Scope of Practice      EMT

 Classification         • Non-opioid analgesic
 Usual
                        • 20mg/2ml ampule
 Presentation
 Mechanism of           • Acts as a serotonin, norepinephrine, and dopamine reuptake inhibitor in the
 Action                   central nervous system, dampening pain signals.

 Indications            • Moderate to severe pain

                        • Known allergy
                        • History of epilepsy
 Contraindications      • Pain associated with STEMI/NSTEMI
                        • Renal Impairment
                        • Pediatrics <13 years
 Precautions            • Nil
                        • Nausea
                        • Vomiting
 Side Effects
                        • Tremors
                        • Dry mouth


 Adult Dose             • 20mg IV (once only, dilute with 100ml Normal Saline and administer as an infusion
 & Administration         over 5 minutes)


 Pediatric Dose
                        • Not for pediatric administration
 & Administration
                        • Pregnancy: Safety has not been established. Nefopam is not recommended for use
 Use in Pregnancy         in pregnancy.
 and Lactation          • Lactation: Safety has not been established. Nefopam is not recommended for use
                          during lactation.

 Additional             • Nefopam must not be administered in the same IV line as Diclofenac as there is a
 Information              chance of precipitation.
