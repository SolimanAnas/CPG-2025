M27                  Midazolam

 Scope of Practice        EP

 Classification          • Benzodiazepine
 Usual
                         • 5mg/1ml ampule
 Presentation
 Mechanism of            • Potentiates the action of the GABA neurotransmitter producing sedation and
 Action                    seizure inhibition
                         • Seizures
 Indications
                         • Sedation of agitated or combative patients

 Contraindications       • Known allergy

                         • Respiratory distress
 Precautions
                         • Shock and/or hypotension
                         • Reduced level of consciousness
 Side Effects            • Respiratory Depression
                         • Hypotension
                         Seizures
                         • 5mg IV/IO/IM (repeat once after 10 minutes if required)
                         Sedation
                         • 1-5mg IV/IO/IM
 Adult Dose
 & Administration
                         Dilution for IV/IO administration
                         • In a 5ml syringe, draw up 4mls of normal saline
                         • Add 1ml (5mg) of Midazolam.
                         • Concentration is now 1mg/1ml
                         Seizures
                         • 0.1mg/kg IV (max 5mg – may repeat once after 10 minutes if required)
                         • 0.2mg/kg IM (max 5mg – may repeat once after 10 minutes if required)
 Pediatric Dose
 & Administration        Dilution for IV administration
                         • In a 5 or 10mL syringe, draw up 4mls of normal saline
                         • Add 1mL (5mg) of Midazolam.
                         • Concentration is now 1mg/1ml
                         • Pregnancy: Fetal harm has been demonstrated however, midazolam should be
