M4                   Aspirin

 Scope of Practice       EMR

 Classification          • Platelet aggregator inhibitor
 Usual
                         • 81mg/tablet
 Presentation
                         • Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid) provides antiplatelet, fever-reducing, and pain-
                           relieving effects.
 Mechanism of
                         • In DCAS, aspirin is used solely for its antiplatelet properties.
 Action
                         • Aspirin blocks the cyclooxygenase enzyme, which leads to a decrease in the
                           production of prostaglandin and thromboxane.

 Indications             • Acute Coronary Syndrome


                         • Known allergy
 Contraindications       • Pregnancy
                         • Active peptic ulcer disease

                         • Asthma/COPD (previous history of worsening bronchospasm after NSAID
                           administration.
 Precautions
                         • Clinically significant active bleeding (e.g. GI bleeding) – aspiring will increase
                           bleeding.


 Side Effects            • Increased bleeding



 Adult Dose              • 324mg (4 tablets).
 & Administration        • Tablets should be chewed before swallowing


 Pediatric Dose
                         • Not to be administered to pediatrics
 & Administration
                         • Pregnancy: as has demonstrated fetal harm in the third trimester.
                                • The likelihood of myocardial infarction or ischemia in pregnancy is very low,
 Use in Pregnancy
                                  aspirin should generally be withheld.
 and Lactation
                         • Lactation: May be administered during pregnancy, the mother should be advised to
                           stop breast feeding until advised by their physician.
                         • If the patient has taken aspirin within the last 60 minutes:
 Additional
                                 • Confirm the dose they have received.
 Information
                                 • Administer additional Aspirin to reach a maximum total dose of 324mg.
