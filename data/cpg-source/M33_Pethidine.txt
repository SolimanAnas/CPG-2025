M33                  Pethidine

 Scope of Practice        EP

 Classification          • Opiate Analgesic
 Usual
                         • 50mg/1ml ampule
 Presentation
 Mechanism of
                         • Binds with Mu opioid pain receptors creating analgesia
 Action

 Indications             • Moderate to Severe Pain

                         • Known allergy
                         • Respiratory depression
                         • SBP <90mmHg
 Contraindications
                         • Renal Impairment
                         • Concurrent use of Mono Amine Oxidase Inhibitors
                         • Suspected bowel obstruction
                         • Respiratory distress or difficulty
                         • Elderly
 Precautions             • Pediatrics age <1 year (increased risk or respiratory depression)
                         • Morbid obesity
                         • Moderate Hypotension / Shock
                         • Respiratory depression
                         • Hypotension
 Side Effects
                         • Drowsiness / reduced level of consciousness
                         • Arrhythmias
                         • IM: 25-100mg (maximum 400mg/24 hours)
                         • IV: 25-50mg. Administer slowly over 2 minutes. (maximum 200mg/24 hours)
 Adult Dose
                         Dilution for IV administration
 & Administration
                         • In a 10ml syringe, draw up 9mls of normal saline
                         • Add 1ml (50mg) of Pethidine.
                         • Concentration is 5mg/1ml (50mg in 10ml)
 Pediatric Dose
                         • IM: 0.5-2mg/kg (repeat after 4 hours)
 & Administration
                         • Pregnancy: May cause fetal harm. Pethidine should not be administered during
                           pregnancy.
 Use in Pregnancy
                         • Lactation: Safety has not been established. Pethidine should only be administered
 and Lactation
                           if the benefit outweighs the risk. If administered, the mother should be advised to
                           stop breast feeding until advised by their physician.

 Additional
                         • Nil
