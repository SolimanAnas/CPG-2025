M17                  Glucose 40% Oral Paste

 Scope of Practice       EMR

 Classification          • Carbohydrate
 Usual
                         • Plastic tube containing 15g glucose
 Presentation
 Mechanism of            • Provides glucose for cellular metabolism, raising blood glucose levels rapidly in
 Action                    hypoglycemia.

 Indications             • Hypoglycemia (RBS<70mg/dl)


                         • Known allergy
 Contraindications
                         • Unresponsive patients or patients unable to swallow


 Precautions             • Airway occlusion in semi-conscious patients (particularly children <2years old)


 Side Effects            • May cause vomiting



 Adult Dose              • 1 tube PO – adjusting amount of paste inserted according to space available.
 & Administration        • Repeat after 5-10 minutes (if required)


 Pediatric Dose          • ½ tube PO – adjusting amount of paste inserted according to space available.
 & Administration        • Repeat after 5-10 minutes (if required)

                         • Pregnancy: Safety has not been established. However, glucose should be
 Use in Pregnancy          administered if clinically indicated.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Safety has not been established. However, glucose should be
                           administered if clinically indicated.
                         • Glucose paste is not absorbed via the buccal mucosa and must be swallowed to
 Additional                be effective.
 Information             • In very young children/neonates, if the patient is able to suck, apply a small amount
                           of glucose paste to the end of a gloved finger and allow the child to suck the paste.
