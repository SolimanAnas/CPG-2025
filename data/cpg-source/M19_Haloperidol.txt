M19                  Haloperidol

 Scope of Practice        EP

 Classification          • Antipsychotic
 Usual
                         • 5mg/1ml ampule
 Presentation
                         • Haloperidol binds to dopamine D1 and D2 receptors in the mesolimbic pathway.
                           This reduces excessive dopamine activity that can cause hallucinations,
 Mechanism of
                           delusions, or severe agitation.
 Action
                         • Haloperidol also affects other histamine receptors leading to sedation, aiding in
                           the management of acute agitation.

 Indications             • Acute psychosis suspected to be secondary to a worsening mental health illness.


                         • Known allergy
 Contraindications       • Parkinsons disease
                         • Dementia

 Precautions             • Nil.
                         • Extrapyramidal effects
                         • Hyperactive behavior
 Side Effects            • Increased muscle tone
                         • Prolonged QT
                         • Torsade's de pointes


 Adult Dose
                         • 5mg IM (IM administration only)
 & Administration


 Pediatric Dose
                         • Not for pediatric use
 & Administration
                         • Pregnancy: Fetal harm has been demonstrated, not recommended for use in
 Use in Pregnancy
                           pregnancy.
 and Lactation
                         • Lactation: Safety has not been established, not recommended for use in lactation.

 Additional
                         • Nil.
