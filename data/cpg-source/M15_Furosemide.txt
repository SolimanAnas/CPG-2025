M15                  Furosemide

 Scope of Practice        P

 Classification          • Loop Diuretic
 Usual
                         • 20mg/2ml ampule
 Presentation
 Mechanism of            • Inhibits sodium reabsorption in the ascending loop of Henle in the kidneys
 Action                    resulting in increased urinary output and fluid excretion.

 Indications             • Acute pulmonary edema with signs of fluid overload


                         • Liver Cirrhosis
 Contraindications
                         • Anuria

 Precautions             • Pulmonary edema without symptoms of fluid overload.
                         • Hypotension
                         • Increased urine output
 Side Effects
                         • Dehydration
                         • Electrolyte imbalances


 Adult Dose
                         • 40mg IV (slow over 1-2 minutes)
 & Administration


 Pediatric Dose
                         • Not for pediatric use
 & Administration
                         • Pregnancy: May cause fetal harm. Administration in the prehospital setting is not
 Use in Pregnancy          advised.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Minimal infant risk, If essential to be administered, the mother should be
                           advised to stop breast feeding until advised by their physician.
                         • Furosemide should be administered only if signs of fluid overload are present.
 Additional              • Administration of non-fluid overloaded patients typically is highly associated with
 Information               in-hospital dehydration and electrolyte imbalances and has not demonstrated
                           significant improvement in patient outcomes.
