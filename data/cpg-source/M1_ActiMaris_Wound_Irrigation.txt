M1                   ActiMaris (Wound Irrigation Solution)

 Scope of Practice         EMR

 Classification           • Topical Antiseptic

 Usual                    • 300mL bottle containing: water, sea salt (1.2%), sodium hypochlorite NaOCl
 Presentation               (0.04%) and hypochlorous acid H0Cl (0.004%).
 Mechanism of
                          • Has an eliminating effect on wound pathogens.
 Action

 Indications              • Irrigation of traumatic and chronic wounds.



 Contraindications        • Known allergy to Actimaris ingredients.


 Precautions              • None Noted


 Side Effects             • Slight, temporary burning sensation around wound area.



 Adult Dose
                          • Use ample amount to provide sufficient wound irrigation.
 & Administration


 Pediatric Dose
                          • Use ample amount to provide sufficient wound irrigation.
 & Administration

 Use in Pregnancy         • Safety has not been demonstrated.
 and Lactation            • Sterile water or saline is preferred if available.

 Additional               • Cross contamination between patients may occur with incorrect handling of
 Information                ActiMaris bottle.
