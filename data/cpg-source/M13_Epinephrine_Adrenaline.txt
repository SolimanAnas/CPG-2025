M13                  Epinephrine (Adrenaline)


                          EMT     • IM Auto Injector (adult and pediatric)
                                  • Prefilled Syringe (1mg/10ml)
 Scope of Practice
                           AP     • Ampule (1mg/1ml)


 Classification          • Adrenergic agonist
                         • IM EpiPen Auto-Injector:
                                 • Adult: 0.3mg IM
 Usual                           • Pediatric: 0.15mg IM
 Presentation            • Prefilled Syringe: 1mg/10ml
                         • Ampule: 1mg/1ml
                         • Note: Presentation in mg, adrenaline infusions in mcg
                         • Adrenaline agonizes adrenergic receptors resulting in:
                               • Bronchodilation
 Mechanism of
                               • Increased heart rate and contractility.
 Action
                               • Vasoconstriction
                         • Adrenaline also directly stabilizes mast cell degranulation in anaphylaxis.
                         • Cardiac Arrest
                         • Shock not compatible with fluid or is fluid refractory
 Indications             • Bradycardia
                         • Anaphylaxis
                         • Life Threatening Asthma

 Contraindications       • None in the emergency context

                         • High risk of arrhythmias
 Precautions             • Adrenaline will increase myocardial oxygen consumption and myocardial
                           ischemia.
                         • Tachycardia
                         • Arrhythmias
 Side Effects
                         • Tremors, anxiety and sweating
                         • Nausea and vomiting
                         Cardiac Arrest:
                         • IV/IO: 1mg every 3-5 minutes
                         Septic Shock:
                         • IV/IO Push Dose: 0.01mg every 2-5 minutes
                         • IV/IO Infusion: 2-10mcg/min
                         Bradycardia:
                         • IV/IO Push Dose: 0.01mg every 2-5 minutes
 Adult Dose              • IV/IO Infusion: 2-10mcg/min
 & Administration        Anaphylaxis
                         • IM 0.3-0.5mg (via auto injector or 1:1,000 ampule)
                         • IV/IO Push Dose: 0.01mg every 2-5 minutes
                         • IV/IO Infusion: 2-10mcg/min
                         Asthma
                         • IM 0.3-0.5mg (via auto injector or 1:1,000 ampule)
                         • IV/IO Push Dose: 0.01mg every 2-5 minutes
                         • IV/IO Infusion: 2-10mcg/min
