M26                  Metoclopramide

 Scope of Practice       P

 Classification          • Antiemetic
 Usual
                         • 10mg/2ml ampule
 Presentation
                         • Antagonizes dopamine-2 receptors in the chemoreceptor trigger zone (CTZ)
 Mechanism of              reducing nausea.
 Action                  • Metoclopramide also (weakly) blocks stimulation of the 5-HT3 receptors in the
                           gastro-intestinal system further reducing nausea and increasing gastric motility.

 Indications             • Nausea and Vomiting

                         • Known allergy
                         • Epilepsy
                         • Parkinsons Disease
                         • Tardive dyskinesia
 Contraindications       • Suspected gastro-intestinal hemorrhage or obstruction
                         • Pheochromocytoma
                         • Recent gastro-intestinal surgery
                         • Age <1 year
                         • Allergy to procainamide
 Precautions             • Pediatrics (extrapyramidal effects)
                         • Extrapyramidal effects (common in children)
                         • Rash
 Side Effects            • Hypotension
                         • Dyspnea
                         • Drowsiness and dizziness


 Adult Dose              • 10mg IV/IM (slow IV over 3 minutes, or diluted in 100ml normal saline and
 & Administration          administered as an infusion)


                         • Generally not recommended for routine pediatric administration (significant risk of
 Pediatric Dose
                           extrapyramidal effects)
 & Administration
                         • 0.1mg/kg IV/IM (slow IV over 3 minutes)
                         • Pregnancy: Safety has not been established. Routine administration in pregnancy
                           is not recommended.
 Use in Pregnancy
                         • Lactation: Safety has not been established. Routine administration in lactation is
 and Lactation
                           not recommended. If essential to be administered, the mother should be advised
                           to stop breast feeding until advised by their physician.
                         • Metoclopramide should not be mixed with morphine.
 Additional
                         • Metoclopramide should not be administered prophylactically after opiate
 Information
                           administration. Only administer if nausea occurs.
