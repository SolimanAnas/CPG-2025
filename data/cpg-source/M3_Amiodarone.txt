M3                   Amiodarone


                             EMT      Cardiac Arrest
 Scope of Practice
                              AP      Infusion for Tachycardia


 Classification          • Antiarrhythmic
 Usual
                         • 150mg/3ml ampule
 Presentation
                         • Amiodarone is a wide-acting antiarrhythmic drug, mainly classified as class III.
                         • It extends the action potential duration, lowers automaticity, and increases the
                           refractory period in atrial, nodal, and ventricular tissues.
 Mechanism of            • Its electrophysiological effects reduce irregular electrical activity (like ectopic
 Action                    beats), slow down electrical conduction, decrease the heart rate, and stabilize the
                           SA and AV nodes.
                         • Amiodarone also slightly reduces myocardial oxygen demand by decreasing
                           inotropy (the strength of heart contractions).
                         • Cardiac Arrest (refractory VT or VT)
 Indications
                         • Rate control/ reversion of wide complex tachyarrhythmias


                         • Known allergy
 Contraindications
                         • VT of VF secondary to Tricyclic Antidepressant Overdose


                         • None if the patient is in cardiac arrest
                         • Hypotension
 Precautions
                         • Sick sinus syndrome (without a pacemaker in place)
                         • Previous 2nd or 3rd degree Heart Block (without a pacemaker in place)
                         • Hypotension
 Side Effects            • Lightheadedness
                         • Arrhythmias – particularly bradyarrhythmia's
                         Cardiac Arrest:
                              • 1st Dose: 300mg IV/IO push
                              • 2nd Dose: 150mg IV/IO push
 Adult Dose
                         Wide Complex Tachycardia:
 & Administration
                              • 150mg/10minutes
                              • Dilute 150mg (3ml) of amiodarone with 100ml D5W and administer it over
                                10 minutes.
                         • Cardiac Arrest:
 Pediatric Dose
                               • 1st Dose: 5mg/kg IV/IO
 & Administration
                               • Dose may be repeated twice (maximum 15mg/kg total)
                         • Pregnancy: fetal harm has been demonstrated. Administration is not
                           recommended unless significantly clinically indicated.
 Use in Pregnancy
                                • Amiodarone should not be withheld in cardiac arrest in pregnancy.
 and Lactation
                         • Lactation: May be administered during pregnancy, however the mother should be
                           advised to stop breast feeding until advised by their physician.
                         • Administer into a large vein where possible.
 Additional              • If hypotension occurs slow or stop the rate of administration.
 Information             • The full dose should be administered even if the rhythm reverts to sinus rhythm
                           (unless hypotension of bradycardia develops)
