M12                  Dopamine

 Scope of Practice       AP

 Classification         • Catecholamine, Positive Inotrope, Vasopressor
 Usual                  • 200mg/5ml ampule
 Presentation           • Note: Ampule presentation in mg, dose calculation in mcg.
                        • Dopamine Stimulates Dopaminergic (D1 & D2), Adrenergic (α1 & ß1) receptors,
                          causing:
                              • At Low Doses (1–5 mcg/kg/min IV/IO):Dopamine Receptors (D1 and D2):
                                increased blood flow to the kidneys and improving urine output.
 Mechanism of
                              • At Medium Doses (5–10 mcg/kg/min IV/IO): Beta-1 Adrenergic Receptors:
 Action
                                increased heart rate and contractility (inotropic effect), improving cardiac
                                output.
                              • At High Doses (10–20 mcg/kg/min IV/IO): Alpha-1 Adrenergic Receptors:
                                causing vasoconstriction, increasing blood pressure.
                        • Hypotension/Shock associated with:
                              • Cardiogenic shock / Congestive Heart Failure
 Indications                  • Bradycardia
                              • Post cardiac arrest
                              • Sepsis


                        • Known allergy
 Contraindications
                        • Pheochromocytoma


 Precautions            • Arrhythmias are associated with high doses required for vasoconstriction
                        • Arrhythmias (particularly with high doses – including VF).
                        • Chest pain
 Side Effects
