M38                  Tranexamic Acid

 Scope of Practice        P

 Classification          • Antifibrinolytic
 Usual
                         • 500mg/5ml ampule
 Presentation
 Mechanism of            • Tranexamic Acid blocks the conversion of plasminogen to plasmin, reducing
 Action                    fibrinolysis, reducing blood loss.
                         • Uncontrolled bleeding secondary to trauma
 Indications
                         • Significant bleeding associated with post partum hemorrhage


                         • Known allergy
 Contraindications
                         • >3 hours since time of injury or start of bleeding


                         • First trimester pregnancy
                         • Renal impairment
 Precautions             • Females taking oral contraceptives
                         • Concurrent thromboembolic disease process
                         • Suspected subarachnoid hemorrhage
                         • Thrombosis
 Side Effects            • Convulsions
                         • Hypotension with rapid injections


 Adult Dose              • 1000mg IV. Once only. Administered within 3 hours since onset of bleeding.
 & Administration        • Dilute in 100ml normal saline, administer over 10 minutes.


 Pediatric Dose          • 15mg/kg IV. Once only.
 & Administration        • Slow IV over 10 minutes.
                         • Pregnancy: Safety has not been established. However tranexamic acid should be
 Use in Pregnancy          administered if clinically indicated.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Safety has not been established. However tranexamic acid should be
                           administered if clinically indicated.

 Additional
                         • Nil.
