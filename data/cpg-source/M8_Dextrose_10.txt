M8                   Dextrose 10%

 Scope of Practice        EMT

 Classification          • Carbohydrate
 Usual                   • 50g in 500ml bottle
 Presentation            • 25g in 250ml bottle
 Mechanism of            • Provides glucose for cellular metabolism, raising blood glucose levels rapidly in
 Action                    hypoglycemia.

 Indications             • Hypoglycemia (RBS<70mg/dl)



 Contraindications       • Hyperglycemia



 Precautions             • Nil


 Side Effects            • Hyperglycemia



 Adult Dose              • 125-250ml (12.5-25g) IV/IO
 & Administration        • May be repeated if hypoglycemia persists


 Pediatric Dose          • 10ml/kg IV/IO (maximum 125ml)
 & Administration        • May be repeated if hypoglycemia persists
                         • Pregnancy: may cause fetal harm. However, should be administered if clinically
 Use in Pregnancy          indicated.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Safety has not been established, However, should be administered if
                           clinically indicated.
                         • To prevent rebound hyperglycemia, avoid excessive doses of Dextrose 10% - titrate
 Additional                the dose based on the patient’s GCS and RBS.
 Information             • If GCS remains abnormal despite normalized GCS, investigate alternative causes
                           of the patients’ condition.
