M34                  Racemic Epinephrine (Adrenaline)

 Scope of Practice       EMT

 Classification          • Adrenergic Agonist
 Usual
                         • 0.5ml nebule containing 2.25% racemic epinephrine
 Presentation
                         • Adrenaline acts on alpha-1 adrenergic receptors in the mucosal blood vessels of
 Mechanism of
                           the larynx and trachea. This causes vasoconstriction, reducing blood flow to the
 Action
                           inflamed tissues and decreasing edema (swelling) in the airway.

 Indications             • Croup



 Contraindications       • Known allergy to racemic epinephrine preparation.


                         • Heart disease or structural abnormalities
 Precautions             • Hypertension
                         • Thyroid disease
                         • Tachycardia
                         • Arrhythmias
                         • Tremors
 Side Effects
                         • Arrhythmias (including VF)
                         • Pulmonary edema
                         • Headache


 Adult Dose
                         • 0.5ml Nebulized (diluted in 3ml normal saline or sterile water)
 & Administration


 Pediatric Dose          • >4 years: 0.5ml Nebulized (diluted in 3ml normal saline or sterile water)
 & Administration        • <4 years: 0.05ml/kg (max 0.5ml, diluted in 3ml normal saline or sterile water)
                         • Pregnancy: Safety has not been established. However racemic epinephrine should
 Use in Pregnancy          be administered when clinically indicated.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Safety has not been established. However racemic epinephrine should
                           be administered when clinically indicated.

 Additional
                         • Nil.
