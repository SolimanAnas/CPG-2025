M11                  Diphenhydramine

 Scope of Practice        EMT

 Classification          • Antihistamine
 Usual
                         • 50mg/1ml vial
 Presentation
 Mechanism of            • Blocks the action of histamine at H1 receptors reducing histamine induced
 Action                    symptoms (such as itching, urticaria and edema)

 Indications             • Moderate to Severe Allergic Reactions / Anaphylaxis.


                         • Known allergy
 Contraindications       • Lactation
                         • Children less than 4 weeks

 Precautions             • Nil

                         • Drowsiness / CNS depression
 Side Effects
                         • Pain associated with IV injection.

                         • 1mg/kg IM/IV (maximum 50mg)
 Adult Dose              • IM administration is preferred.
 & Administration        • For IV administration, dilute in 100ml Normal Saline and administer as an infusion
                           over 5 minutes.
                         • 1mg/kg IM/IV (maximum 50mg)
 Pediatric Dose          • IM administration is preferred.
 & Administration        • For IV administration, dilute in 1ml/kg of Normal Saline and administer as an
                           infusion over 5 minutes.
                         • Pregnancy: Fetal harm has been demonstrated. Should not be routinely
 Use in Pregnancy          administered in pregnancy.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Safety has not been established. Should not be routinely administered in
                           lactation.

 Additional              • IV administration is highly associated with pain at the injection site – avoid
 Information               undiluted administration.
