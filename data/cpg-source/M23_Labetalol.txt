M23                  Labetalol

 Scope of Practice        EP

 Classification          • Beta Blocker
 Usual
                         • 100mg/20ml ampule (5mg/ml solution)
 Presentation
                         • Blocks beta-1 adrenergic receptors in the heart causing a decrease in heart rate
 Mechanism of              and contractility, resulting in reduced blood pressure.
 Action                  • Weakly blocks adrenergic receptors causing vasodilation resulting in reduced
                           blood pressure.
                         • Pre-eclampsia with severe hypertension
 Indications
                         • Eclampsia
                         • Known allergy
                         • Asthma
                         • COPD
 Contraindications       • 2nd or 3rd degree heart block (without pacemaker)
                         • Known sick sinus syndrome (without an internal pacemaker in place)
                         • Patient taking non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers
                         • Heart failure
 Precautions             • 1st degree heart block (may cause bradycardia)
                         • Hypotension
                         • Nausea
 Side Effects
                         • Dizziness
                         • Headache
                         Pre-Eclampsia & Eclampsia
                         • 20mg IV/IO over 2 minutes (maximum 80mg)
                         • May repeat every 10 minutes as required

                         Dilution
 Adult Dose
                         • Due to the large size of the Labetalol ampule, prepare x2 20ml syringes with the
 & Administration
                           same preparation instructions:
                               • In a 20ml syringe, add 12mls of normal saline.
                               • Add 8mls of Labetalol
                               • The syringe concentration is now 40mg/20ml (2mg/ml)
                               • Administer 10mls (20mg) of the preparation over 2 minutes.

                         • Not for pediatric administration

                         • Pregnancy: Safety has not been established. Labetalol should be administered if
 Use in Pregnancy          clinically indicated.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Infant harm is possible. If administered mother should be advised to
                           stop breast feeding until advised by their physician.
                         • The dilution guideline above is intended to maintain cleanliness, minimize
 Additional                contamination risk, and eliminates the need to handle an open ampule if repeat
 Information               dosing is required.
                         • Label both syringes with “Labetalol 2mg/ml”
