M32                  Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)


                            EMR       PO administration
 Scope of Practice
                            EMT       IV/PR administration


 Classification          • Non-opioid analgesic and antipyretic
                         • Oral Tablets: 500mg/1 tablet
 Usual
                         • Rectal Suppositories: 125mg and 250mg/suppository.
 Presentation
                         • Bottle for IV administration: 1000mg/100ml
 Mechanism of            • Paracetamol works primarily by inhibiting prostaglandin synthesis in the central
 Action                    nervous system.
                         • Moderate to Severe Pain
 Indications
                         • Fever >38oC

                         • Known allergy
 Contraindications       • If patient has already taken paracetamol within the last 4 hours, or if the maximum
                           daily dose has already been reached.

 Precautions             • Nil
                         • Rapid administration of IV paracetamol may cause hypotension.
                         • Pruritus
 Side Effects
                         • Nausea
                         • Vomiting


 Adult Dose              • IV/PO: 1000mg (maximum 4g in 24 hours)
 & Administration        • IV doses should be administered slowly over 10 minutes to avoid toxicity.


                         • PR: <5 years: 125mg | >5 years: 250mg
 Pediatric Dose
                         • IV: 10mg/kg
 & Administration
                         • IV doses should be administered slowly over 10-15 minutes to avoid toxicity.
                         • Pregnancy: Safety has not been established. However, paracetamol is generally
                           considered safe to use in pregnancy and may be administered when clinically
 Use in Pregnancy          indicated.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Safety has not been established. However, paracetamol is generally
                           considered safe to use in lactation and may be administered when clinically
                           indicated.
                         • Caution should be utilized when administering IV paracetamol to pediatrics
                           without a syringe driver, infusion pump or micro-drip set due to the inability to
 Additional
                           accurately administer the dose.
 Information
                         • IV administration to pediatrics <33kg is generally not recommended due to the risk
                           of toxicity.
