M24                  Magnesium Sulphate

 Scope of Practice        AP

 Classification          • Electrolyte
 Usual                   • 5g in 10ml ampule
 Presentation            • 500mg in 2ml vial
                         • Reduces bronchial smooth muscle contraction resulting in bronchodilation.
                         • Blocks calcium channels reducing calcium influx into cardiac myocytes, slowing
 Mechanism of              abnormal electrical activity which causes Torsades de Pointes.
 Action                  • Dilates cerebral blood vessels, improving blood flow to the brain and reducing
                           hypertension-related seizures, and as a central nervous system depressant,
                           reducing neuronal excitability.
                         • Asthma (severe or life-threatening)
 Indications             • Eclampsia
                         • Torsades de pointes
