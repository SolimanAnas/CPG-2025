S1                    Scope of Practice Matrix



     DCAS Scope of Practice Matrix
     This scope of practice outlines the medications, skills, procedures, or interventions
     that DCAS staff are authorized to perform.


     Independent Practice
     Interventions or medications marked in a highlighted box may be carried out
     independently without supervision or consultation with Tele-EMS. Note: Tele-EMS
     remains available for guidance if needed.
                                            ER     EMR     EMT          EMT-A   P   AP       EP
      Paracetamol PR/ IV

          In this example, administration of PR or IV paracetamol is authorized for EMT and higher
          clinical level. Emergency Responders and Emergency Medical Responders are not permitted
          to administer PR or IV paracetamol.




     Tele EMS Consultation
     A highlighted box with the Tele-EMS symbol:



     Indicate that the medication or intervention requires prior consultation with Tele-EMS
     before being performed.
                                            ER     EMR     EMT          EMT-A   P    AP      EP
      Diclofenac IM                                          Tele EMS




          In this example, administration of IM Diclofenac is authorized for independent practice for
          EMT-A level and above. EMT’s may administer IM Diclofenac only after consultation with Tele-
          EMS.
