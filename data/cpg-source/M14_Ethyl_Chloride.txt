M14                  Ethyl Chloride (Chloroethane)

 Scope of Practice        EMR

 Classification          • Topical Anesthetic
 Usual
                         • 100ml Chloroethane without propellant bottle
 Presentation
                         • Chloroethane evaporates rapidly, cooling the skin surface, which temporarily
 Mechanism of              desensitizes nerve endings by slowing their ability to transmit pain signals.
 Action                  • May dissolve into nerve membranes, interfering with voltage-gated sodium
                           channels preventing nerve depolarization and blocking pain signals.

 Indications             • Topical anesthesia associated with minor contusions, sprains or IV cannulation.



 Contraindications       • Hypersensitivity to ethyl chloride products



 Precautions             • Nil

                         • Changes in skin pigmentation
 Side Effects
                         • Frosting of the skin causing tissue necrosis


 Adult Dose              • Topically apply for 3-7 seconds from distance of 3-9 inches until just before skin
 & Administration          begins to turn white


 Pediatric Dose          • Topically apply for 3-7 seconds from distance of 3-9 inches until just before skin
 & Administration          begins to turn white

 Use in Pregnancy        • Pregnancy: Safety has not been established. Crossing the placenta is unlikely.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Safety has not been established.

                         • Nil
