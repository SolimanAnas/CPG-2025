M10                  Diclofenac (Voltaren)

 Scope of Practice        EMT-A

 Classification          • Non-Steroidal Anti-inflammatory (NSAID)
 Usual
                         • 75mg/3ml ampule
 Presentation
 Mechanism of             • Inhibits cyclooxygenase (COX-1 and COX-2) enzymes, which stops the production
 Action                     of prostaglandins causing a reduction in pain, inflammation, and fever.

 Indications             • Moderate to Severe Pain

                         • Known allergy
                         • Previous reaction to NSAIDS
 Contraindications
                         • Renal insufficiency
                         • Pregnancy
                         • Asthma/COPD (history of a previous worsening of asthma or COPD after NSAID
 Precautions
                           administration).


 Side Effects            • Pain at injection site



 Adult Dose              • 75mg IM (into a large muscle)
 & Administration        • 75mg IV (diluted into 100ml normal saline, administered over 10minutes)


 Pediatric Dose
                         • Not to be administered to pediatrics
 & Administration
                         • Pregnancy: Fetal harm has been demonstrated. Diclofenac should not be
 Use in Pregnancy          administered.
 and Lactation           • Lactation: Safety has not been established. If essential to be administered, the
                           mother should be advised to stop breast feeding until advised by their physician.

 Additional              • Intravenous administration should be avoided due to an increased risk of
 Information               thromboembolic events.
